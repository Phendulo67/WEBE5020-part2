# Monochrome
A website for a modeling agency 

BRIEF HISTORY
Monochrome is a Modeling agency  that was founded by Blessed Khoza in 2026, the founder of the organization had a goal of uniting people of different races and so from the comfort of his home in Wasbank he started an Instagram page which he used to  showcase the beauty of people as a whole and he achieved this by exclusively posting images in black and white, which in theory was meant to remove any bias regarding skin tone or complexion

THE MISSION 
Monochrome has a clear mission, which is to unite all South Africans and help to eliminate any prejudice or negative stigmas surrounding skin color, showing people that there is beauty in everyone regardless of color, uniting everyone under the banner of black and white
THE VISION
To emotionally empower all South Africans no matter their skin tone or background
TARGET AUDIENCE
Monochrome’s target audience is not limited to a single type of person but opens itself to all people who want to showcase the natural beauty of the South African people to the rest of the world 
CONTACTS AND SOCIAL MEDIA
Instagram
Facebook
Twitter/X
WEBSITE GOALS
The website’s goals are to establish a stronger online presence, provide valuable information, generate more website traffic, and make it easier for more people to contact the modeling agency.
The organization has an average of 100 models in every province and 3 branches in each, including schools.
The website also aims to employ the youth to assist with camera work and photoshoots ,this aims to improve youth unemployment and empower them for a prosperous future 
Technical Requirements & Tools

A responsive five-page modelling agency website using a premium black, white and grey editorial aesthetic.

## Pages
- Home
- About
- Models
- Enquiries
- Contact

## Improvements
- Premium editorial typography and spacing
- Stronger visual hierarchy and page consistency
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
- Collapsing mobile navigation menu
- Animated navigation links and buttons
- Model-card hover effects
- Subtle entrance animation with reduced-motion support
- Improved form styling, labels and autocomplete attributes
- Responsive images using `srcset`, `sizes` and `<picture>`
- SEO-friendly page titles and descriptions
- Professional footer navigation
- Contact page split into clear agency/location cards
- Accessibility improvements including focus states, semantic structure and alt text
